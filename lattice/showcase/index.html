      器 The instruments

      The parts this site is made of. Each control keeps its native behaviour; its state is drawn as a set of dots that maps to the next state. Move the pointer over anything you can press: the cursor's points re-form around it.

      钮 Buttons

      Primary, accent, ghost and disabled. Pressing bursts the cursor's points outward.

      标 Tabs

      The indicator is a swarm. Change tabs and it pours across to the new one.

      相 Switches

      Off, the points drift free as a gas across the whole track. On, they converge and lock into a crystal. The same points, a different phase.

      晶 Progress

      Unfinished work is free-floating gas. Each newly finished part is built from it: the points converge, lock into the lattice with a flash, and stay. Indeterminate progress is a band of order sweeping through. The bar plays by itself; drag the slider to take over.
